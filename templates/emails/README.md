# Example mail drafts

Fictional placeholders only (`<LIKE_THIS>`). Hirepair creates these as **Gmail drafts**; you read, edit and press Send yourself. Rules for every draft: 3-6 short lines, one concrete fact from the CV that matches the job, no invented numbers, no pressure, attachment named `Name_Surname_Role_Cv.pdf`.

---

<ONE_CV_FACT_THAT_MATCHES_THE_JOB>.
My CV is attached; I would be glad to talk for 15 minutes.

<NAME>

## 3. Clarifying question to a candidate (recruiter)

**EN**
Subject: A quick question about your application for <ROLE>

Hello <CANDIDATE_FIRST_NAME>,

Thank you for applying. Your CV does not say how long you worked with <SKILL_OR_TOOL>. Could you tell me roughly how many years and in what kind of project?

<NAME>, <COMPANY>

**TR**
Konu: <ROL> başvurunuz hakkında kısa bir soru

Merhaba <ADAY İSMİ>,

<AD SOYAD>, <ŞİRKET>
